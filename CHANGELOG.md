# v5 (Oct 24, 2026)

- Classes 7–8 and Week 4: seven new skills (S18–S24). They cover competitive equilibrium and residual demand, supply shifts with ε_D and ε_S, Jevons (employment elasticity ε − 1), surplus maximization and ρ, price floors, specific taxes (wedge, incidence, revenue, DWL, Class 8 Figure 6), and shortages and queues.
- Math toolkit: expected value and variance, ahead of the Risk week.
- Knowledge graph: skills list encompassed sub-skills with weights. Review credit flows only along those. Reviews are picked to knock out the most other due reviews, never two from the same skill in a row. Hard skills get their own reviews and reminders.
- Lessons: sittings of at most 4 knowledge points, 2–5 questions each depending on performance. If a point isn't passed in 5, the lesson pauses and is retried later. A second miss assigns refreshers on the key prerequisites first. New lessons avoid back-to-back related skills.
- Timed, interleaved quizzes that adjust toward 80–85% correct. Missed questions get follow-up reviews, then a retake with new problems.
- XP (about 1 per focused minute) and a daily goal. A progress screen shows pass rates per skill and knowledge point, flagging lessons below the 95% target. Well-learned items are typed and timed.
- On-ramp: a Course plan built from the syllabus shows which weeks are in and which await notes. Packs attach to a week and ship their own answer checks, which the app runs before accepting them.
- Hosted-site files: installable (manifest, offline service worker, icons), packs load from `packs/`, and the test suite runs on every push.
- Fixes: progress saves when the app is backgrounded; one checker bug fixed (S1d never tested negative rents; the app was already right); wrong choices no longer give away the answer by shape.
- Ids: all 141 v4 ids kept; 75 added (216 total). v4 progress and v4 backups load unchanged.
